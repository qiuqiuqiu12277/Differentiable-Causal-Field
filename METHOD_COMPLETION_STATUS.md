# Archived implementation checklist

This filename is retained for old links, but the earlier checklist was removed
because it mixed prototype plumbing with unverified paper-completion claims and
referenced a smoke checkpoint that is not present in the repository.

The maintained status is in [REPRODUCIBILITY.md](REPRODUCIBILITY.md). In short:

- the differentiable field, intervention, propagation, and task-head prototype
  are implemented and covered by unit tests;
- the synthetic intervention-coverage diagnostic is runnable on CPU;
- strict split, media, OOD, checkpoint, and free-generation checks are present;
- the complete paper training stack, official manifests, checkpoints, raw
  predictions, and paper-table reproduction artifacts are not bundled.

Do not use this archived filename as evidence that the paper has been fully
reproduced.
